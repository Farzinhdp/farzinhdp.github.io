About

I am an AI Scientist (Senior Staff Data Scientist) at GE Healthcare, working on pre/post training of multi-modal reasoning models for 2D and 3D medical images using VLLMs.

Prior to GE Healthcare, I was a Senior Applied ML Researcher at Biogen, working on generative LLMs for drug discovery using reinforcement learning (RLHF, RLAIF) and DPO. Before that, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Yale University supervised by Amin Karbasi, working on distributionally robust learning, fair ML, and federated learning.

I received my PhD from Pennsylvania State University (2021), supervised by Mehrdad Mahdavi and Viveck Cadambe.
